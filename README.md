# CONSUMER FINANCIAL COMLAINT(CFPB)
## By Abdulsalam Faridat

## Project Overview
This project provides an in-depth analysis of over 62,000+ consumer financial complaints, focusing on complaint distribution, company performance, response speed, customer channels, and geographic patterns.
The analysis was completed using Microsoft Power BI as part of the OnyxData DataDNA Analytics Challenge.

## Aim and Objectives

To determine the total number of consumer complaints recorded.

To analyze how companies respond to complaints.

To examine complaint distribution by company size.

To evaluate complaints across different submission channels.

To identify key trends across product types, regions, and time.

## Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The analysis recorded a total of 62,516 consumer complaints, highlighting the scale of customer dissatisfaction across various financial products and services. One of the

major indicators, the Timely Response Rate, shows that 94% of these complaints were addressed within the required time frame, demonstrating strong compliance by most 

companies. However, despite meeting deadlines, the Average Response Time stood at 15.09 days, meaning companies still take over two weeks on average to resolve issues, 

leaving room for improvement in response speed.

When examining company characteristics, it was evident that large companies dominate complaint volume, receiving the highest number of consumer submissions due to their

wider customer base and market penetration. Additionally, the analysis revealed that over 50% of all complaints were submitted through the Web, making it the most 

frequently used submission channel. This reflects modern consumer behavior, where customers prefer fast, convenient digital platforms to report issues.

## Key Insights & Findings

94% of complaints were responded to on time, but response speed remains slow.

Web is the dominant method of complaint submission.

Checking/Savings products receive the highest number of complaints.

Large companies handle most complaints due to their market share.

California is the leading state for consumer financial complaints.

Complaint volumes rise significantly in Q2 and Q3 each year.
